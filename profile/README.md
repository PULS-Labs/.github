# PULS Group

**Physics Underlying Life Sciences**: theoretical and computational biophysics at
[FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg](https://www.fau.eu) and the [Ruđer Bošković Institute](https://www.irb.hr/eng) in Zagreb.

We combine experiments with statistical physics, theory and simulation to study problems in biophysics and materials science.

## Research areas

- Computational Chemistry
- Biological membranes
- Complex transport
- Molecular modelling
- Tissue development
